The Problem

Cardiac arrest is a race.
Bystanders are losing it.

More than 350,000 Americans experience an out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA) every year — and 90% do not survive. A major contributor is the persistent gap in the quality of bystander-provided CPR.

CPR is only effective when compressions meet guideline targets: 5–6 cm depth at 100–120 compressions per minute. Yet even certified lay rescuers struggle to maintain these parameters under stress and fatigue.

Most cardiac arrests happen at home, where a single bystander is solely responsible — with no team, no monitors, no feedback. The emotional and physical demands are immense, and the gap between perceived and actual CPR competence is well-documented.

The Solution

Place it. Press it.
Save a life.

CPRight is a single-use, wallet-sized CPR feedback patch that provides real-time guidance on chest compression rate and depth. No phone pairing, no app, no external power source — just place it on the chest and start compressions.
